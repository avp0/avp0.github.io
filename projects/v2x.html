V2X Internship: Missile Program Engineering

  • Organization: V2X
  • Period: Summer 2026
  • Relevant Skills: Creo, Mechanical Design, Production Drawings, GD&T, Prototype Assembly, COTS Component Selection, Bills of Materials, Windchill, Manufacturing Coordination, Packaging and Interface Integration

Background

During my V2X internship, I worked on missile program hardware being deployed into the field, supporting mechanical design, manufacturing documentation, and hands-on assembly. My contributions helped move hardware through inspection, fabrication, and integration toward operational use. My work spanned a prototype launcher build, production-model drawings, power distribution hardware, generator integration, and an electronics rack mount redesign for future programs.

Prototype Missile Launcher and Build Support

I helped move a prototype missile launcher from engineering documentation into an assembled system. I produced part drawings to support timely inspection, catalogued commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) components for the bill of materials, and coordinated manufacturing of angle brackets needed for the build. I also designed custom steel brackets to meet customer constraints.

Alongside the drawing work, I supported launcher assembly and integration, and sourced hardware as build needs arose. The launcher was completed in time for a customer demonstration during a site tour, which generated three potential contract leads for future business.

Power Distribution Unit Fabrication

I released production-ready part and assembly drawings in Creo for fabrication of a power distribution unit (PDU) box, addressing the program's MIL-STD-810 environmental requirements. I supported completion of the box, connecting engineering documentation with the fabrication and assembly tasks needed to deliver hardware for the prototype build.

Production Drawings and Documentation

For another missile program, I prepared production-ready part drawings for hardware being prepared for field deployment. I applied dimensioning and geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) to communicate manufacturing and inspection requirements, and managed engineering documentation in Windchill.

Generator Packaging and Custom Fuel Tank/Cell

I evaluated the fit of a new generator within an existing equipment enclosure, resolving packaging and interface constraints for system integration. I also designed a custom fuel tank/cell and associated hardware to meet customer constraints. Together with the custom steel bracket work, this supported on-time delivery of critical hardware.

Electronics Rack Mount Redesign

I redesigned and standardized a server-rack housing and mount assembly for electrical components using COTS 80/20 components, enabling fabrication and reuse across future programs. The assembly incorporated closed gussets, anchor fasteners, and grooved rails, with vibration loads considered during the design work.

Outcomes

  • Supported launcher build and integration for an on-time customer demonstration that generated three potential contract leads.
  • Produced inspection drawings, released production-ready PDU drawings in Creo, and supported completion of the PDU box.
  • Delivered production-ready drawings supporting fabrication and field deployment of missile program hardware.
  • Resolved generator packaging and interface constraints, and designed custom steel brackets and fuel tank/cell hardware to support on-time critical delivery.
  • Standardized a COTS 80/20 server-rack housing and mount assembly for fabrication and reuse across programs.
